About Aptumi

Aptumi.com is a collection of private tools that run 100% in your browser. No file uploads to server.

We started Aptumi because most free tools ask you to upload your resume, passport photo, or thesis to their server. That's risky. Your PII (email, phone, address, NIK) can leak, be stored, or sold.

Our principle: If we don't need your data on server to do the job, we don't collect it. All parsing happens with PDF.js, Mammoth.js, and Canvas API inside your browser. You can verify by opening DevTools > Network - 0 uploads.

Founded in 2026, based in Bandar Lampung, Indonesia, serving global users. One AdSense publisher (pub-8924201509923220) for both mhascan.com and aptumi.com.
