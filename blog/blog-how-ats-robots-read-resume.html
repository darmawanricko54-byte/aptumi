How ATS Robots Read Your Resume (And Why 75% Fail)

Aug 30, 2026 | 7 min read | ATS Guide

Quick Answer: ATS robots read resumes as plain text from left to right, top to bottom. 75% fail because of tables, text boxes, graphics, headers/footers, and scanned PDFs. The fix is simple: use single-column, plain text bullets and test with a private ATS checker (no upload).

When you apply online, your resume doesn't go to a human first. It goes to an ATS (Applicant Tracking System) like Workday, Taleo, or Greenhouse. If ATS can't parse it, you're auto-rejected before a recruiter ever sees you.

How ATS Actually Reads Your Resume (Step-by-Step)

  1. Text Extraction: ATS strips your PDF/DOCX into raw text. If your resume is an image scan, it extracts 0 characters.
  2. Section Detection: Looks for keywords like "Experience", "Education", "Skills". If you use creative headings like "My Journey", ATS misses it.
  3. Keyword Scoring: Compares your text against job description keywords. Needs 70%+ match.
  4. Formatting Check: Tables are read row-by-row left to right, often merging columns into nonsense like "Microsoft Excel2023Google".

Top 5 Reasons Resumes Fail ATS

1. Tables and Columns

Two-column resumes look modern but ATS reads left column then right column as one line. Use single-column.

2. Graphics, Icons, and Skill Bars

ATS sees an image, not "90% in Python". Replace with text: "Python - Advanced".

3. Headers and Footers

Many ATS ignore headers/footers. Your name and contact in header = invisible.

4. Text Boxes and Textures

Canva templates use text boxes. ATS often skips them entirely.

5. Scanned PDF or JPG Resume

If you export from Photoshop as image PDF, ATS extracts zero text.

How to Fix Your Resume to Pass ATS in 2026

  • Use .docx or text-based PDF (not scanned)
  • Single column, simple fonts: Arial, Calibri, Times New Roman
  • No tables, no text boxes, no images
  • Standard headings: "Work Experience", "Education", "Skills"
  • Bullet points with simple dash or dot
  • Save as "FirstName_LastName_Resume.pdf"
